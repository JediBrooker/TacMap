## Explicit assurance limits / remaining owner choices

This is an implementation and regression review against binding documents, not a cryptographic proof, independent penetration-test certification, hardware/OS attestation, or guarantee against root/physical extraction. Android Keystore enforcement, platform TLS/trust and screenshot/biometric policy retain their platform assumptions. Imported originals/generated bakes/user exports remain plaintext; already-started recording retains the same mission DEK until terminal cleanup. Native PDF/SQLite engine CPU/allocation and OS-mediated providers are not fully controlled by app-level budgets. Universal raster ≤1px acceptance remains withheld in Task4. Retention, default-false background reconnect, and distribution/TLS operations stay with the owner.
