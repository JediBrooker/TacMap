REF 06 FAQ

Straight answers.

Do I need a server?
No. Share a join code and your team is synced through an encrypted relay. You can also self-host the relay if you want to own the entire path.
What can the sync relay see?
Ciphertext and routing metadata only. It never sees positions, callsigns, or unit identity, because everything is sealed on your device before it is sent. Details are in the threat model.
Is my data encrypted?
Presence and sync are end-to-end encrypted in transit. On your device, data stays in app-private storage protected by your device lock. Nothing is uploaded to a cloud account.
Which platforms?
iOS and Android, from one codebase, with matching behaviour on both.
Is it really private?
No analytics, no ad networks, no crash telemetry, no account. The repo is open so you can confirm exactly what it does and does not send.
