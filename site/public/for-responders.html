For volunteer search and rescue

For publishers · Threat model · Help · TacMap

TacMap is free for active volunteer search and rescue members. If you turn out for a volunteer SAR unit, bush search team, mountain rescue team or rogaining-based rescue squad, you can have the full app at no cost, on iPhone or Android.

This is a thank you, not a trial. The unlock is permanent and nothing about the app changes: no account, no ads, no analytics, no subscription.

How to get your code

  1. Email christianbrooker@gmail.com with the subject "SAR volunteer code".
  2. Attach a screenshot of your current membership that shows your name and active status. Your unit's member app or portal, a D4H roster entry, or an email sent from your organisation's own domain all work.
  3. Say which platform you use, iPhone or Android. One code per person per platform.

You will have a code within three business days. On iPhone the code opens a redeem sheet in the App Store; on Android you redeem it in Google Play. Both are also reachable from inside TacMap under Settings.

We do not keep your screenshot. Once the code is sent, the attachment is deleted and the only record kept is the date, your organisation, the platform and the last four characters of the code.

Why a SAR unit would use it

  • Your own topo sheets, offline. Import the GeoPDF sheets your agency already issues (NSW Spatial Services, QTopo, Geoscience Australia, LINZ, USGS, OS and others) and they are placed in their own projection and datum, so the on-screen MGRS grid sits on the printed grid. No signal needed in the field. See the GeoPDF guide.
  • Grid references the way you call them. Position readout and entry in MGRS, UTM or latitude and longitude, with a grid overlay, range rings and bearings in degrees or mils. See MGRS on Android.
  • Search assignments as data. Draw segments and lines, drop waypoints and symbols, record tracks, and hand them to the incident management team as GeoJSON, KML or KMZ, or tracks as GPX. They open in QGIS, Google Earth, CalTopo and ATAK.
  • Share with your team without an account. Unit Sync joins devices with a code you share yourself. Positions and markup are encrypted on the device before they leave it; the relay only ever sees ciphertext. The threat model says exactly what it can and cannot see.
  • Night searches. A red night mode and a keep-screen-on setting.
  • Sun and moon times for any point on the map, offline.

TacMap is a navigation and markup tool. It is not a replacement for your agency's official incident management system, and it does not transmit to any coordination centre.

For unit trainers and coordinators

If you run navigation training, a search exercise or a NavShield-style event, we are glad to set a whole unit up at once: a custom code for your iPhone members, a batch of Android codes, and a hands-on session on importing your sheets and joining a Unit Sync room. In return we ask one thing: a short written account of how it went, which you approve before it is published here.

Email the same address with your unit's name, rough headcount by platform, and when you next train.

For national bodies and training organisations

If you publish a recommended navigation toolkit for members, or run navigation courses, the app can be trialled by instructors at no cost. What matters for an organisational decision is usually the data handling, so start with the threat model and the privacy policy: everything stays on the device unless the user turns on an optional online service, and the only network service of ours, the Unit Sync relay, stores encrypted blobs it cannot read.
